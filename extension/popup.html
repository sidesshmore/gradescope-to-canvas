Quiz copier

Gradescope to Canvas, all in your browser.

  1. On a Gradescope rubric page, choose Copy quiz for Canvas and tick the questions you want.
  2. On the Canvas quiz’s Edit page, choose Copy or import, then import.
  3. Not right? Undo an import is in the same place.

The tab is on the right edge of both pages. It can also copy between Canvas quizzes. Titles, settings and publishing are never changed.
